What we ran · what held · what broke

Methods and lessons

A postmortem for scientists and analysts, not a second Method section. Terms are defined on the lay explainers page.

What we actually did

Study 1 is a two-wave critical-incident panel. Time 1 narratives (March 2016) were dual-coded on 13 Rosso facets. Time 2 (February–March 2017) carried well-being, engagement, burnout, and turnover. The interval is about one year (M = 11.58 months). Completers were older and higher in work centrality than noncompleters.

Before any outcome model, we tested whether a language-model panel could replace the human pair. Prompts were written only on the training slice, hashed, and evaluated once on development. No test case was scored while any prompt could still be chosen. No engineered prompt met the standard set for acceptance. Predictors stayed human. The one later test look is described below.

Studies 2 and 3 use Internet Archive snapshots of allnurses.com, captures January 2016 through 8 September 2026: 7,709 posts, 1,453 threads, 4,443 accounts. One model coded every post for meaning events (facet × enacted/frustrated/wished × object named × task fate × emotion × attribution) and four post-level flags; 5,435 events, two-thirds of posts with none. The instrument was written for prompted critical incidents and applied to forum prose without a forum-specific human check: the 200-post transfer sheet was drawn and, by PI decision, not coded. Calls ran at the provider’s default temperature (the lock in the protocol was not applied). Dictionaries cover the full window as archive description. Intervals cluster on thread; account-level resampling was the same or tighter.

What worked

  • Locking the drawers. Train / development / test, with development as a single selection pass, stopped us from turning a failed substitution into a quiet retune.
  • Dimension-specific bars. Agency and others-directed meaning have human ceilings high enough to test against. Communion and self do not. Writing that down before the run was the difference between a failed test and a moving target.
  • False-positive disqualifiers on agreed-absent cells. Every prompt “found” control/autonomy or identity affirmation where both humans had said absent. That is the failure mode that matters for employee text: over-calling meaning.
  • Reporting the registered estimates as they came in, then screening the rest. The competence–strain association would have been easy to promote. Labeling it exploratory, and putting the ~1,600-test ledger in the supplement, is what makes it usable later.
  • Internet Archive rather than a live crawl. The site’s terms forbid systematic retrieval. Snapshots are a completed historical corpus, with capture year stated as the time axis.
  • Parser repair as a data problem, not a collection problem. 2024–2025 posts looked empty because later pages store comments in a different HTML tag. Fixing the extractor recovered the years. No new live collection was added.

What did not work

  • Substitution. Six frozen prompts. All disqualified. Agency PSA peaked at .798 against a .910 bar.
  • Communal meaning as a confirmatory predictor. Human PSA = .48. We excluded it from the confirmatory family before estimation. Unadjusted Time 2 associations were not robust to unresolved cells.
  • Density and curvature. Hypothesis 4 (density to Time 2 burnout) could not be decided in this sample: only 4 of 276 people narrated three or more pathways, so a downturn after the peak cannot be shown. The Time 1 result is different and is in hand: felt meaning rises with density and then flattens (turning point 2.19; slope at 0 positive; slope at 3 not different from zero). True inverted-U effects are rarely identified. Flattening is the form this sample can support.
  • Agency × communion. Sixteen people carried both. The interaction was not estimable.
  • Registered next-year prediction. Six tests; estimates near zero, and three of five single coefficients equivalent to zero at |β| = .30. The joint block added ΔR² = .012. BF01 = 481.5 on next-year meaningfulness.
  • Using later URL paths to recover board names. Newer allnurses links drop the forum slug. Hundreds of threads are unlabeled. Student boards leaked into a working-nurse frame.
  • Treating a one-model forum draft as transfer. It is a draft. The 200-post human sheet was the gate and was waived. Every forum rate is a model-assigned code.
  • Reading a same-post co-occurrence as a lead indicator. Apparatus posts carry quit flags at 15% versus 2%. The reverse conditional is 30%: most quit-flagged posts name no apparatus. One coder, one complaint; not “precedes.”
  • Trusting a series across a template change. The site’s HTML changed in 2019–2021; posts got shorter and codable events dropped. Any year slope through that band carries the caveat, and the frustration slope does not survive it.

Lessons we would hand to another lab

On measurement

Do not set one acceptance number for “meaning.” Agency and belonging are not the same coding problem. If humans cannot see a dimension, a model fitted to those humans will inherit the blindness and look confident.

On models

A published paper that says models beat crowd workers is not a validation of your codebook. The bar is your human pair, on your facets, on your text type. Forum text is a new validation, not a free extra N.

On outcomes

Concurrent convergence (codes with WAMI in the same wave) is not prospective prediction. We had the first and not the second. Report both or you will overclaim the map.

On time

Capture year is not posted date. Posted date is not AI exposure. Hospital adoption is not this writer. A 2023 cut is the wrong era start; the apparatus talk was there in 2016 at the same rate. Nurses do not say “AI”; they say ratios, metrics, charting. Measure the artefact, not the word. None of this is a causal AI test.

The independent panel (Session 6, Tier 3)

This was specified on 11 June 2026, not invented after the one-shot failed. Tier 1 is cells both humans agreed on. Tier 2 is the blinded sample of disagreements. Tier 3 is the panel scored against Time 1 WAMI and Time 2 strain and retention — a criterion no coder, human or model, has seen. Communion human PSA is .48. A prompt selected to match that pair will carry the disagreement forward. A panel that is not selected on human agreement, and is judged on later outcomes, is a different test.

It was not run because ADR 0006 parked all further LLM scoring of the split after every engineered prompt missed the human-agreement bars and the manuscript had a deadline. The park was a shipping decision. The June design still stands. ADR 0010 puts Tier 3 back on the table. After the manuscript results were fixed, one descriptive look at a pre-registered 65-case test subsplit was taken under a hashed protocol (ADR 0012), on PI decision with coauthor deferral; it scored two disqualified prompts and is reported as a 65-case number at development-level precision, not as validation: agency .81 / .80, others-directed .75 / .77, both prompts again disqualified on false positives, salience ICC in the drop band. Same-case human-pair agreement on those 65 was .95 / .84, so the slice was not unusually hard. The pre-registered screen for a later single look at the remaining 128 cases (agency and others-directed both ≥ .75) missed on others-directed at .746; the 128 remain sealed and refused by code. Train was the prompt sandbox. Dev is spent. Twenty-nine narratives sit outside the split (eight with Time 2) and can be scored without an unseal; that is a mechanics check, not the powered test.
